THE PERSON BEHIND THE CODE

A developer,
with a lot of hobbies.

I'm a CMGT student at Hogeschool Rotterdam, with a background in Informatica and an interest in full-stack development. Front-end development and UI/UX are where my love for code and creativity meet.

I enjoy figuring out how an interface can make something complicated feel manageable. My internship at Slik gave me experience working on a real product, while my wardrobe project let me explore how design can support people with ADHD.

HOW I WORK

What’s in my toolbox?

Front end & interaction

Vue 3, TypeScript, HTML, CSS and Less. I enjoy translating ideas into interfaces and refining the small details that make them easier to use.

From interface to data

Experience with NestJS, TypeORM, MySQL, Laravel and PHP. For my wardrobe app, I worked with React Native, Expo and Supabase.

Research & iteration

User research, feedback and testing help me make design choices. I like connecting what people need with what I can build.
