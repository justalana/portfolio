During my third-year internship at Slik, I worked on Offri: a platform for creating proposals. I helped improve the editor through new styling options, interface refinements and fixes across the front end and back end.

Context & role

I worked within an existing product and codebase, contributing to both the Vue front end and the NestJS back end. After finishing my internship, I continued working at Slik part-time.

What I contributed

  • Text styling: text colour support in the Tiptap editor and a colour picker.
  • Block styling: background colours, formatting options and corners, with defaults for quote and reaction blocks.
  • Workspace palettes: reusable colours with a hex value and a name.
  • Consistency and fixes: editor behaviour, image block rules, PDF spacing and back-end validation.

Design meets implementation

These features connected visual decisions to the underlying data. A colour option needed an understandable interface, reliable storage and consistent behaviour when a proposal was rendered or exported. I worked with feedback to refine those details.

What I learned

Working on Offri gave me experience extending a real product, following an existing architecture and responding to review feedback. It strengthened my interest in front-end development and the relationship between implementation and user experience.
